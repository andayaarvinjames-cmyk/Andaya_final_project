Apayao — The Last Ecological Frontier

Apayao is rich in untouched nature and indigenous life. It remains one of the most pristine areas of the Cordilleras, with dense forests, winding rivers, and vibrant Isnag culture.

  • Untouched Nature — Vast forests, rivers, and rich biodiversity
  • Indigenous Culture — Home of the Isnag people and preserved traditions
  • Eco-Adventure — River exploration, wildlife encounters, and off-grid travel

Abra — Heritage, Weaving, and Quiet Landscapes

Abra is known for its rich cultural heritage and peaceful rural landscapes. The province is famous for its abel weaving, historic churches, and serene highland villages.

  • Traditional Weaving — Home of abel textiles and skilled artisans
  • Cultural Heritage — Historic churches and ancestral houses
  • Peaceful Landscapes — Rivers, mountains, and uncrowded destinations

Kalinga — Living Traditions and Sacred Ink

Kalinga is known for its strong indigenous identity and ancient traditions. It is home to communities that continue to practice customs passed down through generations.

  • Traditional Tattoos — Ancient batok art done by hand
  • Living Culture — Strong indigenous traditions and community life
  • Mountain Landscapes — Remote villages and scenic highlands

Mountain Province — Caves, Cliffs, and Culture

Mountain Province is known for its dramatic landscapes and deep-rooted indigenous traditions. The area features famous sites like the Sagada Hanging Coffins and breathtaking mountain views.

  • Cultural Heritage — Home to the famous hanging coffins tradition
  • Natural Wonders — Caves, mountains, and scenic viewpoints
  • Indigenous Traditions — Living customs preserved through generations

Ifugao — Rice Terraces and Living Traditions

Ifugao is home to one of the most iconic cultural landscapes in the world. The Banaue Rice Terraces were carved over 2,000 years ago and remain a living heritage today.

  • Rice Terraces — Ancient hand-carved landscapes still in use
  • Living Culture — Traditions and rituals preserved over centuries
  • Cultural Trekking — Immersive village and farming experiences

Benguet — Highlands, Markets, and Mountain Culture

Benguet is known for its cool climate, agricultural abundance, and vibrant highland culture. It serves as the vegetable basket of Northern Luzon and is home to diverse indigenous communities.

  • Agricultural Hub — Major source of vegetables and strawberries
  • Highland Scenery — Mountains, farms, and cool weather
  • Cultural Diversity — Home to Ibaloi and Kankanaey communities

Baguio — The Summer Capital of the Philippines

Baguio offers a refreshing escape in the mountains with cool weather and cultural charm. The city is known for its parks, markets, festivals, and creative arts scene.

  • Festivals — Home of the colorful Panagbenga Festival
  • Creative City — Arts, crafts, and local talent
  • Cool Climate — A refreshing mountain getaway
